What an entry does and does not mean

It means this address opened a connection to a decoy and did the things recorded against it, at the times recorded. It does not mean the owner did it, that the address is still hostile, or that it should be blocked without your own corroboration — addresses are frequently reassigned and frequently behind NAT.

Absence means very little either. This is one sensor. Not finding an address here says only that this decoy did not see it.

Lyrebird publishes no blocklist, blocks nobody, and sells nothing. If you believe a record is wrong — misattribution, timestamps that cannot correspond to traffic your host originated, or a large-scale NAT egress where the behaviour has stopped — open an issue on the repository with the address and it will be corrected or annotated. A compromised device on your own network is not grounds for withdrawal, but the incident page will carry a dated remediation note on request.
