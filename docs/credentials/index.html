Most common pairs

7,107 distinct pairs across 1,000,711 recorded attempts, of 1,198,841 authentication attempts in total — the difference is attempts where no usable pair was captured.

Only pairs tried at least three times by two or more independent addresses are listed here. A pair guessed by several unrelated bots is a botnet dictionary entry; a single sighting might be a real person's password typed at the wrong host, and that is not ours to publish. What this corpus is for

Check whether your own defaults are in it. Build a deny-list from it. Measure how far a real credential policy sits from what is actually being guessed. What it cannot tell you is whether any of these pairs opened anything: the services were emulated, and acceptance was a decision the decoy made to see what came next.

Attempt counts and distinct pairs are reported separately and never merged; conflating them once overstated this corpus by a wide margin.

Lyrebird

What reaches an ordinary internet address: a continuous record from one honeypot, published as data. Services are emulated; no real host was compromised. Attacker input is reproduced verbatim and is hostile by definition — do not execute anything recorded here.
