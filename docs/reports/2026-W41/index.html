05

Method and caveats

  • One sensor, one location. Absence from this dataset means only that this decoy did not see it.
  • Services are emulated. Nothing was compromised, nothing was blocked, and no attack succeeded against real infrastructure.
  • An address is recorded as observed. NAT and shared hosting mean one address may represent more than one device or person.
  • Country data places the connecting device — usually a compromised consumer router — and is not attribution.
  • Payload hosts are defanged here and are usually themselves compromised third parties.
  • Nothing the sensor said in reply is published, here or anywhere.
